# Demo video script — 60 seconds (record by Nov 2)

Visual: screen recording of the real run on `demo/demo-brief.md`. Narration over it. No face cam needed.

**0–8s** — The brief on screen, clean and confident.
"This is a finished research brief. It reads well. It is also wrong in six places — and nothing in it tells you where."

**8–20s** — Run starts; claims extract.
"9 PHC pulls out every checkable claim and runs nine checks: the sources exist, the sources say what's claimed, the subject under every other name it carries, the numbers rebuilt, the names letter-checked — and then an independent attacker tries to kill what's left."

**20–38s** — Verdicts land, one at a time.
"Designed by Aldus himself? Corrected — the italic was Francesco Griffo's. Never translated into English? Corrected — the translation was hiding under the work's other names. Fifty editions? The items rebuild to forty-seven."

**38–50s** — The Held verdict.
"And this one — the deciding source is a print catalogue from 1932 that can't be opened. So 9 PHC says so: Held, unverified, door named. A checker that can say 'unverified' is the only kind whose 'verified' means anything."

**50–60s** — Report full screen.
"Nine checks. Three verdicts. Receipts for all of them. That's 9 PHC."
