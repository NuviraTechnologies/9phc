9 PHC

9-Point Hallucination Calibration

A post-research audit program. Paste in a finished piece of AI-assisted research — a draft, a report, a set of claims with citations. 9 PHC extracts every checkable claim, runs each one through nine verification checks, and returns a verdict for every claim: Verified, Corrected, or Held — unverified, with receipts. It is not a detector and it is not a guardrail. It audits what was said, fixes what is wrong, and shows the receipts.

Your API key stays in your browser. It is never sent to us, never stored, and never logged — calls go from your browser straight to the Meta Model API.

Provider: the Meta Model API (api.meta.ai) — Muse Spark 1.3 for extraction and the evidence checks, Muse Wonder 1.0 for source fidelity and the adversarial pass. Source retrieval is free and keyless: Crossref, DataCite, Open Library, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar. A typical short research brief (20–27 claims) cost $3.50–$7.20 at the prices recorded in the project's run record — an estimate from that record, not a quote. Every run reports its own calls and tokens, and a 1.5M-token ceiling stops a runaway run.

Demo mode — run the pipeline against a scripted stand-in client. No key needed, nothing is spent, and the output shows the shape of a report, not an audit.

The calibration

Tier note: 9 PHC is the third tier of truth constraint — a post-research audit over finished work. Verdicts rest on peer-reviewed sources and primary records only.

The nine checks

1 · Source existence — every cited source must be shown to exist.
2 · Source fidelity — the cited source, opened, must say what the claim says it says.
3 · Alias sweep — the subject is searched under every other name it carries.
4 · Right-level check — a claim is verified at the level it lives at.
5 · Secondary sweep — later works citing the source are checked.
6 · Own-files sweep — your own files are searched for contradictions.
7 · Numbers recomputed — every total rebuilt from its items, three times. Plain code, no model.
8 · Names and IDs letter-checked — character by character against the source. Plain code, no model.
9 · Adversarial pass — a fresh instance with a debunk mandate tries to kill each claim.

Source standard: peer-reviewed sources and primary records only. Crowd-edited tertiary pages never settle a claim; at most they point toward a door. A claim that cannot be settled is Held, with the unreachable door named — a calibration that cannot say "unverified" cannot make its "verified" mean anything.

What this page cannot do

The full program — the command-line version in the open-source repo — opens source pages directly when a registry record is not enough. A browser cannot do that: most publisher pages refuse cross-origin reads, so this page judges from the registry dossiers (the record, the abstract of record, the catalogue description, the citation graphs). It also keeps no checkpoint: a stopped run starts over. Prompts, checks, gates, and verdict rules are the program's own, unchanged.

If your browser blocks the calls to api.meta.ai (some networks and browser setups refuse cross-origin API calls), the run will report the model endpoint as its door. The fix is the local version: clone the repo, add your key, run it on your own machine.
